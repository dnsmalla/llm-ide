Why local-first

Your data is not the product.

Most AI workspaces upload your meetings, source code and documents to someone else’s cloud. LLM-IDE is built the other way around.

  • Meetings, code, chats and project files stay on your computer.
  • AI runs with the model account you already pay for.
  • The cloud account holds identity and device metadata, not your knowledge base.
  • You decide when an agent may edit, run or publish.

The short version

Cloud tools want your data in their database.

LLM-IDE keeps the database on your desk and lets AI come to it.

Remote phone/browser access is being built as an end-to-end encrypted relay, not a public copy of your project.
