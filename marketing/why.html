The difference

AI that comes to your data.

Cloud AI tools ask you to move your work into their product. LLM-IDE keeps the product around your work.

Built for people who care where the bytes live

Local-first by architecture

The core is loopback-only. The desktop initiates outbound connections; no inbound public core port is required.

One project, many surfaces

Meetings, code, docs, issues and agents share a project instead of living in separate silos.

No AI bill from us

You choose and pay your model provider directly. LLM-IDE does not resell model calls.

Explicit trust boundaries

Approvals, protected paths, local login and a separate account service keep identity away from data.
