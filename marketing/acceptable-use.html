Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Use LLM-IDE lawfully and without harming other people, their systems or the service.

Do not use the service to:

  • Break into, scan or overload systems you do not own or have permission to test.
  • Send spam, phishing, malware or deceptive messages.
  • Access another person’s account, devices or data.
  • Circumvent rate limits, authentication or device approval.
  • Infringe copyright, trade secrets or privacy rights.
  • Harass, threaten or discriminate against people.
  • Violate export, sanctions or other applicable law.

AI and agent use

You are responsible for the prompts you run and the actions you approve. Review code changes and tool calls before applying them. Do not use the workspace to cause damage to systems or data you are not authorised to change.

Enforcement

We may rate-limit, suspend or remove accounts that violate this policy, and may report unlawful activity. If you believe an account is abusive, contact abuse@llm-ide.com.

This is an operational policy draft for the preview, not legal advice.
