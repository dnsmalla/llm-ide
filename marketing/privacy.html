Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. The short version

Your project data stays on your desktop. The account service stores identity, passkey and device information needed to sign in and link devices.

2. What we collect

DataWhyRetention
Email, display name, language, time zoneAccount and communicationUntil account deletion
Passkey public keys and credential metadataSign-inUntil passkey/account deletion
Session and refresh token hashesStay signed in; revoke sessionsSession lifetime
Device labels, type, status and timestampsDevice list and revocationUntil account deletion
IP address in sessions/auditSecurity and abuse prevention72 hours, then nulled
Audit eventsAccount securityUp to 400 days

3. What we do not collect

We do not receive your meeting transcripts, notes, source code, chats, generated documents, provider keys or AI prompts. Those stay on your computer and go to the model provider you choose.

4. Cookies

The account pages use one necessary session cookie. See the Cookie Policy. We do not use advertising or analytics scripts.

5. Sharing

We use infrastructure and email providers to run the service. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

6. Your choices

You can export or delete your account from the account pages. Deleting the account removes identity, passkey, session and device rows; backups age out on the backup schedule.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@llm-ide.com.

This is an operational policy draft for the preview, not legal advice. It should be reviewed for the operator’s jurisdiction before general availability.
