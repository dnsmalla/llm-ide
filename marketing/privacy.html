Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. The short version

LLM-IDE has no account and no login. We run no server that holds your data, and we collect none through this site or the apps.

2. What stays on your computer

Your meeting transcripts, notes, source code, chats, generated documents and provider keys stay on your desktop. AI prompts go to the model provider you choose, under that provider’s terms.

3. Sync (optional)

If you turn sync on, the desktop writes end-to-end encrypted snapshots to storage you choose (MEGA today; your own server over SFTP and Box are coming). They are signed and encrypted on the desktop. That provider sees only ciphertext plus file names, sizes and times, and holds it under its own terms and privacy policy. You can remove a phone or browser from the desktop at any time; the keys rotate.

4. Cookies and analytics

LLM-IDE sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics scripts. See the Cookie Policy.

5. Retired preview accounts

During the preview we ran an account service (accounts.llm-ide.com) for email and passkey sign-in. It is switched off. Any rows it held (email, display name, passkey public keys, device labels) stay on the operator’s server until deleted. If you signed up in the preview, you can ask for deletion at privacy@llm-ide.com.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data held on the retired preview server where required by law.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@llm-ide.com.

This is an operational policy draft for the preview, not legal advice. It should be reviewed for the operator’s jurisdiction before general availability.
