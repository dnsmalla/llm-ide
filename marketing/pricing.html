Pricing

Your tools. Your AI bill.

LLM-IDE is designed around bring-your-own model access. There is no per-token markup from the workspace.

Account

Free while in preview

Account creation, passkeys and device linking are free during the preview. Features and limits may change before general availability.

AI usage

Paid to your provider

You connect your own Claude login or API key. Your provider bills you directly; LLM-IDE does not resell model usage.

Data

No storage upsell

Your project data lives on your computer. The account service does not sell storage for your knowledge base.

What happens at general availability?

Any paid plan will be announced before it applies. Existing preview accounts will be told in advance. We do not believe in silent plan changes or charging for data you already own.
