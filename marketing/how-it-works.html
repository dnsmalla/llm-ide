Work on your desktop

The local core opens the project database, captures meetings and runs AI with your credentials. It binds to loopback, not the internet. No account or login is needed.

  • Local SQLite + project files
  • Your Claude login / API key
  • Approvals and protected paths
Loopback only

Encrypt before it leaves

When you turn sync on, the desktop writes snapshots of your meetings, plans and chats. It signs and encrypts them on the desktop.

  • Keys stay on the desktop
  • Signed, so changes are detected
  • Nothing readable leaves the machine
Signed + encrypted

Keep it in your own storage

Snapshots go to storage you own: MEGA today, with your own server over SFTP and Box coming. The provider sees only ciphertext.

  • Free 20 GB on MEGA
  • No LLM-IDE server holds your data
  • Under your provider’s terms
No plaintext

Use the workspace anywhere

Link a phone or browser by scanning a QR or opening a link, then confirm the same 6-digit code on both screens. It reads the last snapshot even when the desktop is off and queues prompts the desktop answers later.

  • QR or link + 6-digit code
  • Remove a device at any time; keys rotate
  • Reads offline, queues prompts
Linked by QR

Four steps to a project

1 · Install

Install the desktop app. It is free and needs no account or login.

2 · Open a project

Open the project folder, capture a meeting or connect a source.

3 · Link your phone or browser

Optional: turn on encrypted sync, then scan the QR and confirm the 6-digit code.

4 · Use it anywhere

Chat, review and plan with the project in context, on the desktop or from your phone.
