From account to workspace

How LLM-IDE works.

1. Create your account

Your account is for identity and linking devices. It stores your email, display name and passkeys — not your meetings, code or chats.

2. Run LLM-IDE on your desktop

The desktop app holds the project, the local database and your model credentials. The local core binds to loopback, so it is not reachable from the internet.

3. Connect a browser or phone

A browser or phone is approved by the desktop. Until the encrypted relay is enabled, the web client can connect only through the explicitly configured desktop host.

4. Work with AI

Chat, capture, review and automation use the model provider you configure. The cloud account makes no AI calls and has no AI bill.

Data path

You → desktop workspace → local project data.

AI → your model provider, with the context you choose.

Account service → identity, passkeys, device list and (when enabled) encrypted relay envelopes.

The account service never receives meeting transcripts, source files or chat history.

What you need

  • A desktop computer for the workspace.
  • A passkey-capable browser or device for account sign-in.
  • Your own Claude login or provider API key for AI features.
